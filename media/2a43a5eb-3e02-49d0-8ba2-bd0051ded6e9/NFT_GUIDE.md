## NFT Knowledge for the Agent
- ERC-721 = unique NFT standard (1 token = 1 item)
- ERC-1155 = multi-token standard (editions, semi-fungible)
- Floor price = lowest listed price for any NFT in a collection
- Floor sweeping = buying multiple NFTs at/near floor price
- Sniping = buying underpriced NFTs quickly before others
- Rarity sniping = targeting specific rare traits
- Blue chip = high-value, established collections (Bored Apes, Punks)
- Wash trading = fake volume to manipulate perception (detect and warn)
