# CryptoVerse AI — Events Guide (for Events Director Agent)

## Event Types
| Type | Duration | Has Score | Has Prize | Description |
|------|----------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Weekend Challenge** | 48 hours | Yes | Yes | Short trading blitz — highest P&L wins |
| **Monthly Tournament** | 30 days | Yes | Yes | Month-long points accumulation |
| **Team Battle** | Event-defined | Yes | Yes | 5-person teams compete — aggregate score |
| **Live Webinar** | Session-based | No | No | Educational live-stream with experts |
| **Market Analysis** | Event-defined | Yes | Yes | Submit prediction — closest to reality wins |
| **Flash Challenge** | 1-6 hours | Yes | Yes | Ultra-short intense trading challenge |

## Event Status Lifecycle
| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **scheduled** | Event is announced, registration open, not yet started |
| **active** | Event is live — participants competing |
| **completed** | Event finished — winners determined, prizes distributed |
| **cancelled** | Event cancelled before or during execution |

## Weekend Challenge Details
- **Duration:** 48 hours (Saturday 00:00 UTC to Sunday 23:59 UTC)
- **Scoring:** Highest simulated P&L (profit and loss) from trading
- **Rules:** Fresh virtual balance for fairness (or uses existing balance)
- **Prizes:** CP coins awarded to top 3 participants
- **Style:** Fast-paced, aggressive trading encouraged within risk bounds

## Monthly Tournament Details
- **Duration:** Full calendar month
- **Scoring:** Points system based on:
  - Profit consistency (daily P&L)
  - Risk management (low drawdown bonus)
  - Trade frequency (active participation)
  - Win rate
- **Prizes:** Significant CP rewards + leaderboard prestige
- **Style:** Consistent, disciplined trading rewarded over single big wins

## Team Battle Details
- **Teams:** 5 members each
- **Scoring:** Aggregate team score = sum of individual member scores
- **Strategy:** Teams can coordinate strategies
- **Prizes:** Team-wide CP rewards + special badges
- **Style:** Collaborative competition

## Flash Challenge Details
- **Duration:** 1-6 hours (announced with short notice)
- **Scoring:** Highest P&L in the time window
- **Style:** Extreme intensity, test of quick decision-making
- **Prizes:** Quick CP rewards

## Leaderboard System
- Real-time rankings during active events
- Tracks: P&L, win rate, total trades, consistency score
- Global leaderboard for all-time rankings
- Event-specific leaderboards for each competition

## Prize Distribution
- Prizes paid in **CP coins** (in-app currency)
- Distribution happens within 24 hours of event completion
- Tiered rewards: 1st place gets largest share
- Minimum participation threshold may apply

## Fair Play Rules
- No collusion between participants
- No multi-accounting (one user, one account)
- No exploitation of simulation bugs
- Suspicious patterns automatically flagged
- AI Mentor can detect but NOT modify outcomes
- Results are final once verified

## Competition Management
- Events Director (AI) can:
  - Organize and schedule competitions
  - Announce upcoming events
  - Answer questions about rules and scoring
  - Check leaderboard standings
  - Identify and flag suspicious trading patterns
  - Announce winners based on leaderboard data
- Events Director CANNOT:
  - Change trade results or balances
  - Modify competition outcomes
  - Override verified results

## AI Events Director Rules
- Explain all event types and rules clearly
- Motivate participation without creating FOMO
- Help users join teams for Team Battles
- Check leaderboard standings on request
- Explain prize distribution and claiming process
- Detect and report suspicious patterns
- Announce winners after verification
- NEVER modify results — they are system-verified
- Remind users: competitions are simulations for education
