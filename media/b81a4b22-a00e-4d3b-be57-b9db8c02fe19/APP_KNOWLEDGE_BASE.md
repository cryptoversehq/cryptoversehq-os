# CryptoVerse AI - Knowledge Base for AI Mentor

## Platform Overview
CryptoVerse AI is a comprehensive crypto trading simulation and education platform.

## Core Features

### 1. Trading Simulator
- Users start with $100,000 virtual USD
- Support for: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, DOGE, MATIC, DOT, LINK
- Order types: Market, Limit, Stop-Limit
- Leverage: 1x to 100x (based on user level)
- Stop Loss and Take Profit orders
- Real-time P&L calculation

### 2. Academy
- 8 modules with 4 lessons each (32 lessons total)
- Modules: Blockchain Basics, Market Analysis, Risk Management, DeFi & Advanced Trading, On-Chain Analysis, NFT Fundamentals, Sentiment Trading, Automated Trading
- Each lesson includes: content + quiz (4 questions, 80%+ required to pass)
- XP system: earn XP by completing lessons and successful trades
- Levels: Novice → Apprentice → Analyst → Pro Trader

### 3. Strategy Marketplace
- Users can buy/sell trading strategies
- Price range: Free to 1,000 CP
- 20% platform fee on sales
- Creators earn 80% of sales price

### 4. Copy Trading
- Follow top traders with configurable copy percentage (25%-100%)
- Copy fees: 2% to 10% of profit (set by trader)
- Risk limits: max daily loss, stop loss for copied trades

### 5. On-Chain Analysis
- Support for: Ethereum, Bitcoin, BNB Chain, Solana, Polygon
- Whale transaction alerts
- Smart money wallet tracking
- Exchange flow analysis

### 6. NFT Analytics
- Support for: OpenSea, Blur, LooksRare, Magic Eden, Rarible
- Collection floor prices, volume, sales
- Rarity score calculation
- Simulated NFT trading

### 7. Sentiment Analysis
- Fear & Greed Index
- Twitter/Reddit sentiment analysis
- News sentiment analysis
- Trading signals based on sentiment

### 8. Live Events & Competitions
- Weekend Warrior (48h challenge)
- Monthly Championship (30-day)
- Team Battles (5-person teams)
- Live Webinars
- Flash Challenges (1-6 hours)

### 9. Wallet & Payments
- Virtual USD balance
- CP coins (in-app currency)
- Subscription plans: Bronze (free), Silver ($9.99/mo), Gold ($19.99/mo), Platinum ($29.99/mo)

## Common Issues & Solutions
1. "Can't place order": Check if you have sufficient balance or open positions limit
2. "Price not updating": Wait 5 seconds or refresh the page
3. "Login failed": Use "Forgot Password" to reset
4. "Strategy not saving": Ensure you have completed the backtest
5. "Copy trading not working": Check if you are following a trader with active trades
